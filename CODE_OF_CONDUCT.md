# Code of Conduct

XyoLua GameForge adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) as its code of conduct. The summary below is not a replacement for the full text.

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and assume good intent.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the community and the project.
- Take responsibility for mistakes and learn from them.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome attention.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions, chats — and when someone represents the project in public.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior to the maintainers through GitHub: open a [private security advisory](https://github.com/xyoluastudios/xyolua-gameforge/security/advisories/new) titled "Code of Conduct report", or contact a maintainer directly. Reports are handled confidentially.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that violate this code, and may warn, temporarily ban or permanently ban contributors, following the enforcement guidelines of the Contributor Covenant 2.1.
